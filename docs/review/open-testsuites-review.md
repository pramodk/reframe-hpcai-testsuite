Prioritize reusable coverage in this order: GPU smoke/burn and device checks; NCCL/RCCL and MPI collectives; PyTorch/TensorFlow distributed training smoke tests; scheduler/job-launch checks; container runtime checks; parallel filesystem I/O; then broader application benchmarks such as GROMACS, CP2K, LAMMPS, HPL/HPCG, and STREAM.
